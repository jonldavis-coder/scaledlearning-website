Are these real prices?

What is the member knowledge library?

A shared, searchable collection of lessons learned, how-tos and insights that members contribute from their own work, circles and cohorts, and draw from when they need it. Contributions are reviewed before they're published.

Is the magazine really included?

Yes. Every paid membership includes ScaledLearning Magazine every month.

Are certificates accredited?

No. ScaledLearning certificates show you completed a ScaledLearning learning path. They are not an industry credential or accreditation.
