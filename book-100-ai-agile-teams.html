100 AI-Agile Teams

A playbook of 100 agile team patterns for pairing people with AI agents

Agile teams will soon include AI agents, but there is no single right way to combine them. One hundred practical AI-agile team patterns, from a solo expert with a research agent to whole service teams with agent crews, each with roles, handoffs, oversight and signs that it is working.
