1:1 executive & transformation coaching

For leaders running a scalable-learning or AI transformation. We work on your real challenges, build learning rhythms into your organization, and help you lead by asking better questions.

  • Video sessions, 3- and 6-month packages
  • Async support between sessions
  • Personal learning log captured as you go

Group coaching

Six to ten peers, facilitated twice a month. Bring live problems, learn from each other's experience, and leave with commitments to act on. Takeaways are added to the group's shared notes.

  • Twice-monthly live sessions
  • Peer accountability
  • Shared knowledge notes

Small peer circles led by a trained facilitator. Members share, seek and capture know-how together. Circles feed the member knowledge library, so every insight can help someone else.

  • Monthly live circle + async thread
  • Trained ScaledLearning facilitator
  • Discounted for members

Train to run ScaledLearning mentoring circles, learning retros and workshops inside your organization or independently. Includes a cohort, a practical assessment and a delivery license.

How it works

Simple to start

  1. Discovery call. A free 30-minute conversation about your goals.
  2. Pick a format. 1:1, group, circle or certification. Whatever fits your situation.
  3. Learn on real work. Every session starts from a live challenge and ends with an action to take.
  4. Capture and compound. Lessons go into your learning log and, if you choose, the shared knowledge library.
