Project management, the comic book.

ACT Comic Series is a pocket-size monthly comic (5.5 × 8.5 in) that pokes fun at project management, agile development and the big hype, drawn in the style of a vintage 1940s Golden Age comic. Because sometimes the best retro is a good laugh.

The recurring cast

Cast preview

The Scrum Master

Nine ceremonies a day and a calendar that has achieved sentience. Believes any problem can be solved with one more meeting.

The PMO

Keeper of 47 dashboards, each tracking the other 46. Has never once been asked what the project is actually for.

The Hype Consultant

Arrives by slide deck. Promises total transformation by Friday. Leaves before anyone checks.

The Robot

Sits quietly in the corner and does the real work. Nobody has noticed yet.

Cast preview: characters and descriptions may change as the series develops. Any resemblance to your last steering committee is purely coincidental.
