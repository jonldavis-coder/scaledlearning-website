Where Innovation Ideas Come From

At 33 degrees, just above freezing, the ice begins to give and the water starts to move. Ideas work the same way. A short, practical look at where innovative ideas really come from: the moment of thaw when frozen assumptions loosen, conversations warm up and new thinking starts to flow, and how teams and AI can create more of those moments.
