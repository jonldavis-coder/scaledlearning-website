Resource hub

Tools for learning that scales.

Change efforts fail in the people and the learning, long before they fail in the technology. Know-how stays in one team, agility turns into ceremony, and agentic AI arrives before anyone has shared the context it needs. This hub is a set of canvases, templates, checks and reading for closing that gap.

Canvases

Put the plan on one page

A canvas is a shared page for a conversation that would otherwise wander. It keeps a team honest about the learning goal, the risks, and what good will look like before anyone starts building.

Self-paced

AI Adoption Canvas

A one-page canvas for planning a responsible AI pilot with a clear learning goal. It asks what you hope to learn, who is affected, which data the pilot touches, and how you will know the experiment worked.

Facilitation notes sit with the canvas, so a lead can run the conversation without turning it into a slide deck. Use it before a pilot starts, not after the tool has already been chosen.

Learning aids

Templates and worksheets for the week ahead

Learning at scale does not wait for a course to finish. These kits are the worksheets, playbooks and rhythms a team can open on Monday: capture what you learned, run a retro, coordinate many teams, or facilitate the session yourself.

Self-paced

Scalable Learning Playbook

A step-by-step playbook for building learning rhythms into everyday work. It lays out how a team seeks a lesson, shares it, captures it and applies it, so learning is a habit rather than an event.

The formats are in Notion, Google Docs and PowerPoint, ready to copy into the tools you already use. Use it when learning is still something people do after the real work.

Lessons-Learned Capture Kit

Lightweight templates for capturing tacit know-how from retros, cohorts, meetups and boot camps. Each one asks for the lesson, the situation it came from, and where someone else can reuse it.

The full kit is the templates plus a short guide. A free sampler comes with the offer, so you can try the capture format before you rely on it with a whole team.

Learning Retro Kit

Ready-to-run retro formats that turn a piece of work into shared learning. They fit a short retro or a longer after-action review, and they end with a lesson someone else can find later.

Decks are in Miro and PowerPoint. Use them when the team did the work but the learning walked out with the people in the room.

Agility at Scale Operating Rhythm

Templates for coordinating many teams without adding bureaucracy. The rhythm covers how teams share what they are learning, where decisions sit, and how a dependency gets resolved without a new committee.

It is a set of templates, not a framework rollout. Use it when several teams need one pace and the process is starting to outweigh the learning.

Assessments

See where learning is thin

A tool will not tell you whether people are learning. These checks ask about reflection, captured lessons, peer sharing, and whether leaders make room for experiments. Start with the free quick check. It is scored entirely in your browser, and nothing is stored or sent.

AI-Readiness quick check

Six quick questions, about two minutes. You'll get an example score and a suggested next step. It's scored entirely in your browser, and nothing is stored or sent.

Free

1. Do teams regularly reflect on their work (retros, after-action reviews)?
2. Are lessons learned and how-tos captured somewhere people can find them?
3. Do people share know-how peer to peer across teams?
4. Have you run at least one AI pilot with a clear learning goal?
5. Is there clear guidance on responsible AI use and data privacy?
6. Do leaders encourage questions and experiments over having all the answers?
Assessment

Learning-Agility Assessment

An individual and team profile of how readily people seek, share and apply what they learn. It is a longer look than the two-minute check, for one person or for a team that wants the same questions answered together.

You get a profile you can discuss, not a ranking. Use it when a team suspects it is busy and still not learning from the work.
